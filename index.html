Clipper — a personal clipping tool for the “Salomon SIDIBE” YouTube channel

Clipper is a private desktop tool used only by its author. It turns long-form videos for which the author owns the rights (podcasts, interviews, trainings) into short horizontal clips of 2 to 5 minutes, and publishes the clips the author approves on the author's own YouTube channel.

Clipper uses YouTube API Services.

What it does

  • Transcribes the author's source videos locally and proposes clip candidates for review.
  • Uploads approved clips to the author's own channel as private videos with a scheduled publication date, with title, description, tags, thumbnail and playlist.
  • Reads the views and average view duration of those clips to measure their performance.

Clipper is not distributed, not sold, and never accesses other users' channels or data.
